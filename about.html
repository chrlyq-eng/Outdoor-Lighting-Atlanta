About our studio

Outdoor lighting designed to make your property shine.

We specialize in custom outdoor lighting systems for Atlanta homes, patios, landscapes, and businesses. Our goal is simple: create a brighter, safer, more beautiful exterior without overwhelming the natural character of your property.

Our approach

Elegant design. Dependable installation. Real results.

Atlanta weather brings intense heat, summer storms, and long evenings, so outdoor lighting needs to be durable, functional, and thoughtfully designed. We build systems using premium fixtures, weather-resistant materials, and energy-efficient LEDs that stand up to the local climate.

From subtle pathway lighting to security upgrades around entrances and driveways, we balance beauty with practicality. Your design is based on your architecture, landscaping, and how you use the spaces around your home.

Every installation is performed with care, from fixture placement to wiring and final calibration. We focus on lighting that looks refined at night and remains easy to maintain for years.

  • Custom lighting plans tailored to each property
  • Warm, low-glare lighting for curb appeal and comfort
  • Security-focused install solutions for entrances and walkways
  • Professional fixture selection and clean installation
